## About Me

I am a PhD student at the Weizmann Institute of Science studying the molecular mechanisms that regulate gene expression, with a focus on translation control.

My research investigates how the ribosome's mRNA entry channel participates in translational regulation and how cellular proteins interact with this region to influence translation, cellular stress responses, and innate immunity.

## Research Interests

- Translational regulation and control of gene expression
- Ribosome structure and function
- Ribosomal protein interactions with mRNA and regulatory proteins
- Cellular and viral mechanisms of translation control
- Translation and innate immune responses

## Goals for This Course

Through this course, I aim to develop practical skills in Python, version control with Git and GitHub, and reproducible computational workflows. I hope to apply these skills to biological data analysis and to improve the reproducibility and efficiency of my research.

## Future Plans

I plan to continue investigating how ribosomal interactions regulate translation and how these mechanisms contribute to cellular responses to stress and infection. I am also interested in expanding my computational skills to support experimental research and data analysis.
